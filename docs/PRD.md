# PRD - Land Negotiator

Problem: land offers arrive as unstructured text and are answered by intuition or by spreadsheets with hidden
assumptions. The costliest mistake in development is overpaying for land or buying a risk that could have been avoided.
User: primarily a developer with a defined investment line (thesis); also lenders, family offices and brokers, land acquisition analysts, family offices, brokers needing a defensible first answer before spending on DD.
Job: decide GO / BORDERLINE / NO-GO and the maximum bid, show how payment structure changes it, and list what to ask and verify.
Principles: the practitioner's rules, tagged by provenance; vocation-specific protocols; no invented prices where the
criterion is not documented; AI argues, code computes; demo data fictitious.
Out of scope: taxes in the decision (by rule), permanent debt sizing, multi-phase master plans, automatic comparables homologation.
